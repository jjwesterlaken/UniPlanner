Privacy Policy

University Planner · Last updated 12 August 2026

University Planner is a study planner for university students. This policy describes what the app actually stores, where it is stored, and which other companies see any of it. It is written to match how the software behaves rather than to describe an ideal, and it includes the parts that are inconvenient to say.

The short version. Your planner is stored in Australia. There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics of any kind, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. The AI features are the only part that sends anything overseas, and only when you choose to use them: a lecture you record, or a piece of text you paste in or have already written and ask the AI to work on. A recording is deleted as soon as it has been transcribed, and text you supply is never stored at all. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Who we are

University Planner is an independent app operated from Australia. For anything in this policy, including access and deletion requests, contact privacy@uniplannerapp.com.

What we collect

If you use the app without an account

Nothing reaches us. Everything you type is stored in your own browser or device and never leaves it. You can use the whole planner this way.

Your account

Creating an account stores your email address and a securely hashed password, handled by our authentication provider. We never see your password. Accounts also carry a plan level (free or ai) and the date the account was created.

Your planner

Once you have an account, your planner syncs so it follows you between devices. That is a single record holding what you have entered: courses, assignments, to-dos, readings, calendar events, notes, study cards, assessment marks and your study statistics.

One thing is stored beside it rather than inside it. The full text of a saved AI lecture note — the summary and any translation — is kept in a separate record, because those notes are large and the planner is sent in full every time it syncs. Your planner keeps the note's title, its course and week, and the first line or so for the list. It is the same data, held by us in the same place under the same protections, and it is yours on the same terms: it stays until you delete the note or delete your account. We mention it only because saying “your planner holds everything you write” would no longer be exactly true.

AI features

All of these are optional, and none of them runs unless you choose it. You accept a separate consent screen before using any of them.

  • AI lecture notes. Records audio of a lecture, has it transcribed, and turns the transcript into structured notes.
  • AI features that work on text you supply. Some features send text you give them — a note you wrote, your study cards, an explanation you have typed, or a section of a reading you paste in — so the AI can summarise it, generate practice questions from it, or tell you what your explanation missed. It does not matter where the text came from: it is relayed to the AI to do the thing you asked and is not stored by us, not in your planner and not on our server. Only the result is saved, and only if you save it. No audio is involved, and nothing is sent unless you ask for it on that piece of work.

We record how much of your monthly AI allowance you have used, so it can be enforced. That is a running total, not a record of what you asked for.

The three things AI notes creates, and how long each lasts

These have genuinely different lifetimes, and conflating them would be misleading, so they are set out separately.

WhatWhere it livesHow long
The recording A private storage area only you can read Deleted the moment transcription succeeds. It is never kept. If a recording fails before that point, it is deleted automatically within an hour.
Our copy of the transcript and generated notes Our database, in Australia 7 days, so you can recover them if something goes wrong mid-way. 30 days if we managed to transcribe your lecture but failed to write the summary — because that used your AI minutes, and the transcript is the only thing you have to show for it.
The notes saved into your planner Your planner record and your device, with the full text of a saved lecture note in a separate record of your own (see “Your planner” above) Yours. They stay until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting the note deletes both halves; deleting your account deletes all of it.

Worth being explicit about. That second row means a complete second copy of every transcript and every generated summary sits on our server for 7 or 30 days, even though you think of the notes as living in your planner. It exists so a failed or interrupted recording can be recovered, and it is deleted automatically on the schedule above.

Where your data is stored

Your account, your planner, your saved AI lecture notes and our temporary copy of any AI notes are stored with Supabase in Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2). For an Australian student that is the answer to the question most people mean: your notes are held in Australia, under Australian law.

Storage is not the same as processing, and we would rather say so than let the sentence above imply otherwise. Two things do leave the country:

The AI features send two kinds of thing overseas. If you record a lecture, your recording goes to a transcription service and the resulting transcript text goes to a summarisation service. If you use an AI feature on text you supply, the text you chose — a note, some study cards, an explanation you typed, or a section of a reading you pasted in — goes to the same summarisation service. It is relayed and not retained: unlike a lecture, where we keep the transcript for a short window so you can get it back, text you supply has no server-side copy at any point.

Both services are operated by companies based in the United States, and every request is made from our server rather than from your device. Nothing else in the app is sent overseas, and none of it happens unless you use an AI feature.

What the app keeps on your device

Two things, both stored by your browser or the app itself, and neither of them sent anywhere except as described above.

  • A copy of your planner. This is what lets the app work offline and before you have an account at all. Signing out leaves it in place deliberately — it is your planner, and it is the same copy someone using the app without an account has.
  • Lecture notes you have opened, kept for offline reading. Because the full text of a saved lecture note is no longer carried around inside your planner, notes you have opened are kept on the device so they still work on a train or in a building with no signal. This is cleared when you sign out, and when you delete your account. Unlike the planner copy, it is not left behind on a shared or family computer.

Neither is a tracking mechanism, neither is readable by any other website, and clearing your browser's data for this site removes both.

What we never do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics and no tracking of any kind. There is no Google Analytics, no tracking pixel, no third-party script and no behavioural logging in the app. The only server the app itself contacts is our own.
  • No selling or sharing your data with anyone for marketing.
  • No reading your notes for training. We do not use your content to train AI models, and text sent to an AI feature is sent to answer that one request — it is not kept by us afterwards beyond what the table above describes.

Two things about security you should know

Both of these are true, neither is obvious from using the app, and we would rather you learned them here than assumed otherwise.

  • Your planner is not end-to-end encrypted. It is protected in transit and access is restricted so that no other user can read it, but it is stored in a form that someone with administrative access to the database could read. If something is sensitive enough that this matters to you, it does not belong in a study planner.
  • The offline demo mode does not check passwords. If the app is running without a server connection, the sign-in screen accepts any password, because there is nowhere secure on your own device to check one. That mode stores everything locally and syncs nothing. It is there so the app works before an account exists, and it should not be treated as securing anything.

Your choices and your rights

  • See your data. Everything the app holds about you is visible in the app.
  • Export it. The Account tab has a backup button that downloads your whole planner as a file you keep.
  • Correct it. Everything is editable in the app.
  • Delete it. Account → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Ask us anything. privacy@uniplannerapp.com. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children

University Planner is intended for university students. It is not directed at children, it does not ask for your age, and nothing in it behaves differently depending on how old you are. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and — where the change affects AI lecture notes — you will be asked to read and accept the updated consent wording before recording again.
