The planner itself is free and has no limits. The AI features run on credits. One credit is one minute of recorded lecture, which is the unit everything else is priced against, so a text action costs whatever it costs in lecture-minutes rather than in a currency of its own.

Every plan includes the whole planner and syncing across your devices. What a paid plan buys is AI credits, and nothing else is held back from the free plan.

The free 60 credits are a trial rather than a monthly allowance. They are granted once for the life of the account and do not come back at the start of a month.

When credits reset

On a paid plan, your credits reset to the full amount at the start of each calendar month — 00:00 UTC on the 1st — whichever day of the month you subscribed. Subscribing on the 28th gives you that month's full allowance for the rest of the month, and a fresh full allowance on the 1st. The reset is not tied to your billing date.

Unused credits do not carry over

Each month starts fresh at the full amount, and anything you did not spend is gone. A longer term does not pool either: six months of Study AI Max is 3,000 credits in each of those six months, not 18,000 available at once.

What a credit does not guarantee

Credits buy AI processing, not a particular result. The AI features are generated by machine and can be incomplete or wrong, so check anything that matters against your own notes and the source material. Where a request fails before any processing happens, nothing is charged. Where processing really did run and the result could not be used, it is charged, and the app tells you so rather than charging quietly.

Paid plans are sold as subscriptions for a term of 1 month, 6 months or 12 months. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each term, at the price then published for that plan, until it is cancelled. Prices are shown before you buy, in your local currency where the store provides one.

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the term you have already paid for, and you keep full access until then — cancelling does not cut you off mid-term and does not refund the remainder of the term by itself. After that the account returns to the Free plan, with your planner untouched.

Where a renewal payment fails, the plan stays active while the payment is retried. If it never succeeds, the subscription ends and the account returns to Free.

Where you cancel depends on where you bought:

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Closing your University Planner account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google either, because those subscriptions are held by the store rather than by us — cancel it with the store as well.
