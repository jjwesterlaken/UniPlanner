HIST2104 — Major Essay (2,000 words) — Marking Criteria

ARGUMENT (40%)
  A clear thesis, stated early and sustained through every paragraph.
  The essay takes a position and defends it, rather than surveying.
  Counter-evidence is engaged with rather than omitted.

EVIDENCE (30%)
  Specific examples, with dates and figures where the claim requires them.
  Sources are attributed. Quotations and borrowed formulations are
  identified as such.
  Generalisations are proportionate to the evidence offered for them.

STRUCTURE (20%)
  Paragraphs are signposted and follow one another for a reason.
  The reader can see at each point why they are being told this.

EXPRESSION (10%)
  Key terms are defined at first use.
  Prose is clear; claims are not repeated for emphasis.

Bands: High Distinction 85+, Distinction 75-84, Credit 65-74,
Pass 50-64, Fail below 50.
